# Privacy Policy

**SoroSoro**
Last updated: March 2026

---

## Overview

SoroSoro is designed with privacy as a default, not an afterthought. The app does not collect, transmit, or share your personal data. Everything stays on your device.

---

## Data We Collect

**None.**

SoroSoro does not collect any personal information, usage data, analytics, or identifiers. We have no servers, no accounts, and no backend.

---

## Data Stored on Your Device

All content you create in SoroSoro — including entries, settings, themes, and history — is stored locally on your iPhone using AsyncStorage. This data:

- Never leaves your device
- Is not transmitted to any server
- Is not accessible to us or any third party
- Is removed when you delete the app

---

## Notifications

If you enable notifications, SoroSoro uses iOS's local notification system to schedule reminders based on the deadlines and recurring items you set. These notifications are generated entirely on-device. No data is sent to external servers to deliver them.

---

## Third-Party Services

SoroSoro does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services.

---

## Children's Privacy

SoroSoro does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

---

## Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top of this page and note the change in the App Store release notes.
