Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 22, 2026

Pocket Cellar stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Introduction

Pocket Cellar ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal wine cellar management application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All data you enter into Pocket Cellar is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to any server. This includes:

  • Wine collection data: Cellar names, wine details (name, type, vintage, winery, region, grape variety, price, notes, and quantity)
  • Wine photos: Photos you attach to wine entries, stored in the app's local storage
  • App settings: Your preferences including language, theme, and currency settings

No Data Transmitted

Pocket Cellar is a fully offline app. We do not operate any servers, do not collect analytics, and do not transmit any data over the internet. Your wine collection is entirely private.

Permissions We Request

Photo Library Access

With your permission, the app may access your photo library to let you attach photos to wine entries. Photos are stored locally within the app and are never uploaded anywhere.

Camera Access

With your permission, the app may use your device camera to take photos for wine entries. These photos are stored locally and never transmitted.

Third-Party Services

Pocket Cellar does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. No third party has access to any data you enter into the app.

Data Security

Your data is stored locally on your device and protected by your device's built-in security features (such as passcode and Face ID / Touch ID). We recommend keeping your device software up to date to maintain the best security.

Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by:

  • Deleting individual wine entries or cellars within the app
  • Uninstalling the app, which removes all locally stored data from your device

Children's Privacy

Pocket Cellar is not intended for use by children under the age of 17. The app involves content related to alcoholic beverages and is designed for adult wine collectors.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
